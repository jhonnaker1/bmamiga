Amiberry
========

BMAmiga's emulation is Amiberry 8.4.1 (https://github.com/BlitterStudio/amiberry),
by Dimitris Panokostas and its contributors, built on WinUAE by Toni Wilen
and UAE by Bernd Schmidt and others: its libretro core, built unmodified
into the kernels with BMAmiga's platform layer (src/port/). Amiberry is
under the GNU General Public License version 3 (LICENSE.txt in the root of
the card); the parts of it from UAE and WinUAE under the GNU General Public
License version 2 or later (GPL-2.0.txt).

Amiberry includes code from other projects, which is in the kernels too.
Their notices follow, as they are in Amiberry's source.
