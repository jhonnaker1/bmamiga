Amiga Kickstart ROMs
====================

The Amiga's Kickstart ROMs are copyrighted (by Cloanto today), so they
are not included: copy your own into this folder. Without any, the Amiga
starts with AROS, the free replacement Kickstart that comes with
Amiberry; it runs much Amiga software, but not all of it.

Amiberry recognises a Kickstart by its contents, whatever its name, so
the files from Amiga Forever (amiga-os-130.rom, amiga-os-310-a1200.rom
and so on, with their rom.key, which must be here too) work as they are.
It also looks for these names first, for each model (F12, Model):

  Amiga 500 (Kickstart 1.3)     kick13.rom   (or kick.rom, kick12.rom)
  Amiga 500 (Kickstart 1.2)     kick12.rom
  Amiga 500+ (Kickstart 2.04)   kick20.rom   (or kick204.rom)
  Amiga 600 (Kickstart 2.05)    kick205.rom  (or kick20.rom, kick31.rom)
  Amiga 1200                    kick31.rom   (or kick40068.A1200)
  Amiga 4000                    kick31.rom   (or kick40068.A4000)
  CD32                          cd32.rom     (or amiga-os-310-cd32.rom),
                                and cd32ext.rom (amiga-ext-310-cd32.rom)
  CDTV                          cdtv.rom     (or kick34005.CDTV),
                                and cdtvext.rom

Kickstarts can be bought with Amiga Forever (https://www.amigaforever.com)
or copied from your own Amiga.
